🦜 Parakit — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Parakit ("the app") is an offline-first expense and debt tracking application developed by Mehmet Fatih İçin. Your privacy is fundamental to how Parakit is designed.

Data We Collect

We collect no personal data. Parakit does not require an account, login, or registration. All data you enter (transactions, categories, contacts, ledger entries, settings) is stored only on your device. We have no servers and cannot see, access, or transmit your financial records.

Advertising

The free version of Parakit shows ads served through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource). On iOS, personalized ads are shown only if you grant permission in the App Tracking Transparency prompt; otherwise ads are non-personalized. The ad network may process limited device information (such as device model, advertising identifier where permitted, and coarse ad-serving data) to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. See Unity's Privacy Policy for details. Purchasing Parakit Premium removes all ads.

Your ad choices (EEA, UK, Switzerland and US): If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Parakit asks for your consent before ads may be personalized. If you choose non-personalized ads, you will still see ads, but the ad network is told not to use your personal data for targeting. If you are in the United States, choosing non-personalized ads also opts you out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information for targeted advertising under US state privacy laws (such as the CCPA/CPRA), and we pass this "do not sell or share" signal to Unity LevelPlay. You can change your choice anytime in the app under Settings > Ad privacy options. The legal basis for personalized ads in the EEA/UK is your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time.

In-App Purchases

Premium is offered as a one-time purchase processed entirely by Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see or store your payment details.

Notifications

If you set a due date on a ledger entry, Parakit schedules a local reminder notification on your device. Notifications are generated locally and never leave your device.

Microphone & Voice Input

Parakit offers an optional voice entry feature. When you tap the microphone button, your device's built-in speech-recognition service converts your speech to text so a transaction can be added. Parakit does not record, store, or send this audio to its own servers. Microphone access is used only while you are actively dictating.

Data Backup & Deletion

Your data stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app. Any backup/export feature creates files only where you choose to save them.

Children

Parakit is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from any user.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address.
