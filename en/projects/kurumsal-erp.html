Corporate ERP

An in-house ERP that brings restoration, staff, fixed-asset and finance processes into one system.

Role
Sole developer
Stack
.NET, SQL Server, Oracle, SGB–MYS

I brought restoration, staff, fixed-asset and finance processes, previously run separately by different departments, into a single system. Departments work on the same data, approvals flow through the system and reports come from one source. Fixed-asset and financial records are integrated with the public financial management systems (SGB–MYS).

My role: Sole developer: analysis, development and go-live.

Technologies: C#, .NET, SQL Server, Oracle, REST API, SGB–MYS integration
