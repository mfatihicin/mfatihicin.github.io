For more than 15 years I have designed and built the ticketing, access control, agency, ERP and staff systems of palaces and museums at the National Palaces Administration of the Presidency of Türkiye, as sole developer.

The heart of my work is bringing different worlds together on one reliable foundation: .NET Core services, SQL Server and Oracle databases, government and banking integrations, and hardware such as turnstiles, kiosks and RFID. Outside the institution, I build my own mobile products with the same care.

Putting AI to work

AI adds value to enterprise systems only with the right data, permissions and oversight. Where I focus:

Search and Q&A over enterprise data

Assistants that answer from document systems and databases, filtered by each user's permissions (RAG).

An AI layer on existing APIs

Adding classification, summarisation and form filling to existing .NET services, with an audit trail.

Privacy first

Masking personal data, minimising what reaches the model and running models locally when needed, in line with KVKK and GDPR.
