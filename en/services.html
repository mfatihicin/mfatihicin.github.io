FAQ

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. I work remotely in English or Turkish; I am based in Istanbul (UTC+3).

Do you take on small jobs?

Yes. Limited-scope work such as fixing one slow query or a small integration is fine.

How is pricing done?

You get a written quote, either fixed price or hourly, depending on the scope.

Do you sign an NDA?

Yes. We can sign a non-disclosure agreement before we start talking details; anything you share is used only for that project.

Who owns the source code?

Unless the proposal says otherwise, the source code and rights to the delivered work are yours. The code lives in your repository; I don't build closed setups you would depend on me for.

Do you need access to our real data?

As little as possible. I prefer anonymised or test data; if real data is needed, the scope and duration of access are agreed in writing beforehand, and personal data is handled in line with KVKK and GDPR.

What happens after delivery?

Bug fixes are free for the period stated in the proposal. After that we can agree on new features or regular maintenance separately.

How many projects do you take on at once?

Only a few, so each one gets the attention it needs. We agree on a start date in the proposal.
