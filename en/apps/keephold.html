KeepHold: Photo Vault & Password Locker

Move anything you don't want in your gallery into an encrypted vault. Photos, documents, notes and passwords — all on-device with AES-256.

App Store▶ Google Play

Features

Photo & video vault

Move media out of your gallery; folders and editing.

Document vault

View and edit PDFs and text files.

Encrypted notebook

Notes with checklists and tables.

Password manager

2FA (TOTP) codes and breach checks.

Strong locks

PIN, password, pattern, Face ID and a panic lock.

Backup & transfer

Encrypted backups and phone-to-phone migration.

FAQ

Are my files uploaded to a cloud?

No. Files are encrypted with AES-256 and stay on your device; no account or server is needed.

What if I forget my passcode?

Use the recovery key created during setup to regain access.

Is there an Android version?

Yes — on Android the app is called Kasam and is available on Google Play.
