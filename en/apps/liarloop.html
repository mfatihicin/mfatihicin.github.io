FAQ

How many players?

3 to 20 players.

Can we play offline?

Yes. In One Phone mode you pass a single device around without internet.

How does the imposter game work?

Everyone except the imposter gets the same secret word. Players take turns giving clues, then vote for who they think the imposter is.
