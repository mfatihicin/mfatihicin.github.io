The Turkish coffeehouse table on your phone — without gambling: Okey, Backgammon, Batak, Pişti, King and 101 Okey against smart bots, offline.

Features

Classic table games

Backgammon, Okey, 101 Okey, Batak, King and Pişti with real rules.

Smart bots

Easy, Normal and Hard; bots are always clearly labelled.

Offline

Play anywhere and resume unfinished tables.

Party games

Name-City, Taboo-style Anlat Bakalım and Imposter.

Word & puzzle

Daily word, Sudoku, Solitaire, 2048, Minesweeper and more.
