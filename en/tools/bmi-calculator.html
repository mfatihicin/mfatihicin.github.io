BMI ranges

According to the World Health Organization, below 18.5 is underweight, 18.5–24.9 normal, 25–29.9 overweight and 30 or above obese. BMI ignores muscle mass and age; talk to a doctor about health decisions.

Frequently asked questions

Is BMI accurate for athletes?

It can be misleading for people with high muscle mass.

Can it be used for children?

No. Children are assessed with age and sex-specific percentile charts.
