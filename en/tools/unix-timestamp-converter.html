What is Unix time?

Unix time is the number of seconds since 1 January 1970 00:00 UTC. It is independent of time zones, which makes it popular in databases and APIs. Some environments such as JavaScript use milliseconds.

Frequently asked questions

What is a 13-digit value?

A timestamp in milliseconds; the tool detects it automatically.

What is the year 2038 problem?

Seconds stored in a 32-bit integer overflow on 19 January 2038; 64-bit systems are not affected.
