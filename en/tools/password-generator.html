What makes a password strong?

Length matters most. A random 16-character password with letters, digits and symbols has more than 100 bits of entropy. Use a different password for every site and keep them in a password manager.

Frequently asked questions

Are the passwords stored?

No, they are generated and shown only in your browser.

How long should a password be?

At least 14–16 random characters for important accounts.
