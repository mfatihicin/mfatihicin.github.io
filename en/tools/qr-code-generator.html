How to use a Wi-Fi QR code

Choose “Wi-Fi network”, enter the network name and password, then download and print the code. Guests scan it with their phone camera and join without typing the password. Ideal for cafés, offices and homes.

Do these QR codes expire?

No. Some sites route QR codes through their own short links, which stop working when a subscription ends. Codes made here carry the information directly, so they keep working forever.

Printing tips

  • Print at least 2×2 cm; go larger if it will be scanned from a distance.
  • Leave a white margin around the code.
  • Always test with your phone before printing.
