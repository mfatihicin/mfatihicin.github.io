What is the Stroop effect?

Reading is automatic, so when a word's meaning and its colour differ, naming the colour takes longer. The test is used to measure attention and focus.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good score?

25 or more correct in 30 seconds is very good.

Can colour-blind people play?

The test may be misleading for people with red–green colour blindness.
