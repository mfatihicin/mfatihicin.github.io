How to merge PDFs

  1. Add files with “Choose PDF files” (you can add more in several rounds).
  2. Use the arrows to set the order.
  3. Press “Merge and download” to save a single PDF.

Are my documents safe?

Many online PDF tools upload your files to their servers. Here, merging is done in your browser with the open-source pdf-lib library, so ID copies, contracts or bank statements never leave your device.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free?

Yes, completely free with no watermark or page limit.

Can I merge password-protected PDFs?

PDFs that need a password to open cannot be read. Remove the password first and try again.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Everything runs in your browser; files are not sent anywhere.
