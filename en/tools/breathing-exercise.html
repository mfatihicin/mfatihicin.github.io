The 4-7-8 technique

Breathe in through the nose for 4 seconds, hold for 7, and breathe out slowly through the mouth for 8. In box breathing every phase lasts 4 seconds. Stop if you feel dizzy, and ask a doctor if you have a medical condition.

Frequently asked questions

How many rounds?

Four rounds is a good start.

When does it help?

Before an exam or meeting, or to wind down before sleep.
