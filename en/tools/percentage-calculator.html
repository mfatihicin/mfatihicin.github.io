Formulas

  • X% of a number: number × X ÷ 100
  • A as a percent of B: A ÷ B × 100
  • Change: (new − old) ÷ old × 100
  • Discounted price: price × (1 − discount ÷ 100)

Successive percentages do not add up: +20% then −20% takes 100 to 96, not back to 100.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate a pay rise percentage?

(New salary − old salary) ÷ old salary × 100.

How do I take 15% off?

Multiply the price by 0.85.
