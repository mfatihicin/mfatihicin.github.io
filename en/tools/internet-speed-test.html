How to read your results

Download affects streaming, browsing and downloads; about 25 Mbps per person is enough for 4K video. Upload matters for video calls, cloud backup and live streaming. Ping is the round-trip time: under 30 ms is great for gaming and calls, over 100 ms feels slow. Jitter shows how much ping fluctuates; lower means a steadier connection.

Why am I not getting the speed I pay for?

  • Wi-Fi loses a lot through walls, distance and the 2.4 GHz band; test with a cable.
  • Other devices may be downloading in the background.
  • Evening peak hours can slow shared networks.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good internet speed?

25–50 Mbps is comfortable for one person. Busy households with 4K streaming and gaming benefit from 100 Mbps or more.

Should I test over cable or Wi-Fi?

Cable. Connect your computer to the router with an Ethernet cable for the most accurate result; Wi-Fi results are affected by the environment.
