Why encode URLs?

Spaces, non-ASCII letters and characters such as &, ? and # have special meaning in addresses. When they are part of a parameter value they must be percent-encoded, for example %20 for a space.

Frequently asked questions

What does %20 mean?

It is the percent-encoded form of a space.

Why are no parameters shown?

Parsing needs a full address starting with https://.
