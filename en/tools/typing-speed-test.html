Average typing speed

Most people type 35–45 words per minute; touch typists often reach 60–80. Accuracy matters more than raw speed.

Frequently asked questions

How is WPM calculated?

Correct characters are divided by five to count words, then scaled to one minute.

Does it work on phones?

Yes, though results are usually lower than on a physical keyboard.
